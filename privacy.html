The phone number on this page is a connection line. Your phone carrier and the call-routing platform may process caller ID or phone number, call time, duration, routing details, and the information you choose to say about your project or location. Call recording, retention, and further use are controlled by the call-routing platform and the provider you reach, subject to their notices and applicable law. Listen to any notice given during the call and avoid sharing payment details, government ID numbers, or other sensitive information unless it is necessary and you trust the recipient. Electrical Service Connections does not control independent providers or your carrier.

The page itself does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. Your browser, hosting provider, or third-party content provider may process technical data as part of delivering and protecting their services. You can use your browser settings to manage cookies and other storage.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or needed to address security, fraud, or abuse.

We do not operate a web form or account that stores information submitted through this page. Privacy rights depend on where you live and which organization holds the information. If you make a privacy request, identify the organization that handled your call and contact it directly. Do not include sensitive information in a public message.
